# GLOBAL PUBLISHING ADMINISTRATION AGREEMENT

**Tunewave Globel Network**  
**Effective Date:** September 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 2026

## 1. Purpose
This Global Publishing Administration Agreement (“Publishing Agreement”) appoints Tunewave Globel Network as administrator of the publishing rights and share expressly submitted and authorized by the Rights Holder.

## 2. Ownership
Rights Holder retains ownership of its compositions and underlying works. Tunewave receives administration rights only for the applicable administered publishing share and Services.

Master/sound-recording ownership and master distribution rights are not transferred by this Publishing Agreement unless a separate agreement expressly provides otherwise.

## 3. Publishing Rights
Where applicable, administration may cover mechanical/reproduction rights, public performance/communication rights, digital publishing uses, UGC/platform-native uses, micro-sync, lyric licensing or monetization, print, live-performance income, retroactive/unmatched/unclaimed royalties and other publishing income expressly included in the activated Services.

Commercial synchronization licensing and neighboring rights may be optional and subject to separate activation or terms.

## 4. Appointment
During the applicable Term and Territory, Rights Holder appoints Tunewave as the exclusive administrator of the administered publishing share, except for rights and arrangements expressly excluded or reserved in the applicable commercial terms.

Rights Holder must not knowingly create conflicting publishing administration or collection appointments for the same administered share during the applicable exclusive period.

## 5. Territory
The standard Territory is worldwide unless otherwise stated in accepted commercial terms.

## 6. Registration and Administration
Tunewave may register compositions, maintain metadata, submit claims, administer society relationships, work with sub-publishers and collection partners, collect royalties, process unmatched or retroactive income and perform related administration.

Tunewave may decline or suspend administration where ownership, contributor splits, registrations or chain-of-title information is incomplete or conflicting.

## 7. Required Information
Rights Holder should provide accurate writer, composer and lyricist information, ownership splits, IPI/CAE information where available, ISWC/registration details where available, society information, ISRC references where relevant, prior publisher information and other chain-of-title documents reasonably required.

## 8. Term
Unless different commercial terms apply, the standard initial term is three years. Unless otherwise agreed, the agreement may renew for successive one-year periods unless either party provides at least 90 days' written notice of non-renewal.

Convenience termination during a fixed term may be limited by the applicable commercial terms.

## 9. Breach and Immediate Termination
Tunewave may terminate or suspend the agreement for material breach, fraud, false ownership or split information, conflicting rights, infringement, loss of rights, unlawful conduct, material society/platform risk or repeated failure to provide required rights documentation.

Where a breach is capable of cure, a reasonable cure period may be provided, generally 30 days.

## 10. Revenue Share
Unless different accepted commercial terms apply, Rights Holder receives 70% of Net Receipts attributable to the administered publishing share and Tunewave retains 30% as its administration share.

Statutory or non-waivable author/composer shares that cannot lawfully be administered are excluded from this calculation.

## 11. Net Receipts
Net Receipts means amounts actually received and retained for the administered share after applicable third-party commissions, taxes, collection costs, reversals, chargebacks, society deductions and other permitted adjustments.

## 12. Direct Receipts
If Rights Holder receives amounts attributable to the administered share that were generated through Tunewave's administration work, Rights Holder must disclose the receipt and pay Tunewave its applicable administration share within 30 days after receiving an applicable invoice or payment request.

## 13. Statements and Payments
Tunewave generally provides quarterly royalty reporting where source statements and receipts have been received. Processing and payment timing depends on society, sub-publisher, platform and other third-party reporting cycles, as well as KYC, tax and payment requirements.

## 14. Reserves and Adjustments
Tunewave may reserve, withhold, suspend or offset amounts for ownership disputes, split conflicts, duplicate registrations, society disputes, fraud, reversals, chargebacks, source corrections, tax/KYC requirements and material operational risk.

## 15. No Guaranteed Collection
Tunewave will use commercially reasonable efforts to administer and collect applicable publishing income but does not guarantee a minimum royalty, society registration outcome, retroactive recovery, synchronization placement, licensing opportunity or collection timeline.

## 16. Rights and Ownership Warranties
Rights Holder represents that it owns or validly controls the administered share, has authority to appoint Tunewave, has obtained necessary contributor approvals and has disclosed relevant existing publishing arrangements and conflicts.

## 17. AI and Synthetic Content
Rights Holder must not submit or represent as authorized any AI-generated or synthetic musical, compositional, melodic or lyrical authorship elements where doing so would infringe third-party rights, violate applicable law, society rules, platform policies or contractual obligations.

## 18. Enforcement
Tunewave may coordinate reasonable rights-management, claims and enforcement activities relating to administered rights. External enforcement costs may be deducted from recoveries where permitted by the applicable commercial terms.

## 19. Reversion
Upon valid expiry or termination, administration rights revert as provided by this Agreement and applicable law, but existing registrations, licenses, claims, collection cycles and accrued income may continue to be administered or reconciled until properly completed.

## 20. Promotion
Unless the Rights Holder has opted out in writing where an opt-out is available, Tunewave may use titles, writer credits, authorized artwork, authorized biographical information, lyric excerpts and limited authorized reference material for non-commercial catalogue promotion and service marketing. No master ownership is transferred by this permission.

## 21. Confidentiality
Each party will use reasonable measures to protect confidential business and rights information received from the other party, subject to disclosures required by law or necessary to perform the Services.

## 22. Liability
To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, indirect and consequential losses are excluded. Subject to applicable law and contractual exceptions, aggregate liability is generally limited to the Publisher/Administration Share actually retained by Tunewave from the relevant administered rights during the twelve months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

## 23. Data Protection
Personal information is processed in accordance with the Tunewave Privacy Policy and, where applicable, a separate Data Processing Agreement.

## 24. Sub-Publishing and Delegation
Tunewave may appoint collection societies, sub-publishers, administrators, agents and other authorized partners where reasonably necessary to administer the rights.

## 25. Force Majeure
Neither party will be responsible for delay caused by circumstances beyond reasonable control, subject to applicable law and any specific termination rights stated in the commercial terms.

## 26. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. The parties will attempt good-faith resolution before formal proceedings. Where arbitration applies, it will be conducted under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, with the seat in Jaipur, Rajasthan, unless a separate written agreement provides otherwise.

## 27. Entire Agreement
This Publishing Agreement, accepted commercial terms, enabled service modules, catalogue submissions and related onboarding records form the agreement concerning the applicable publishing administration Services. Separate master distribution or other agreements are not replaced unless expressly stated.
