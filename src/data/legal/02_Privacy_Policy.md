# PRIVACY POLICY

**Tunewave Globel Network**  
**Effective Date:** September 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 2026

## 1. Scope
This Privacy Policy explains how Tunewave Globel Network collects, uses, stores, shares and protects personal information in connection with its website, dashboard, applications, APIs, distribution, publishing, monetization, royalty and support Services.

## 2. Information We Collect
We may collect:
- Name, email, phone number and contact information.
- Account, artist, label, publisher and profile information.
- Billing, tax, payout, invoice and transaction information.
- PAN, GST and other information required for legal, tax, KYC or payment purposes.
- Music and rights information including audio, video, lyrics, compositions, metadata, ISRC/UPC, splits, ownership information, artwork, registrations, claims and royalty information.
- Technical information such as IP address, device, browser, operating system, access logs, API activity and diagnostics.
- Cookie and similar technology information.
- Communications and support records.

## 3. How We Use Information
We may use information to:
- Create and manage accounts.
- Deliver music and rights-management Services.
- Process publishing registrations and royalty collections.
- Operate YouTube, Content ID and social monetization Services.
- Process payments, taxes, KYC and royalty reporting.
- Provide customer support.
- Detect fraud, artificial streaming, unauthorized access and abuse.
- Maintain security and Platform integrity.
- Analyze and improve Services.
- Communicate operational, legal and service information.
- Comply with applicable law and lawful requests.

## 4. Sharing Information
We may share relevant information with DSPs, YouTube, social platforms, collection societies, PROs/CMOs, sub-publishers, payment providers, cloud and infrastructure providers, analytics providers, fraud/security providers, communication providers, professional advisers and governmental or legal authorities where necessary.

We do not sell or rent personal information as a commercial data product.

## 5. International Processing
Because music distribution and rights administration are international Services, information may be processed or stored in India and other countries where our service providers, platforms, societies or business partners operate. We use appropriate safeguards required by applicable law.

## 6. Data Retention
We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for Services, contractual obligations, royalty accounting, publishing administration, tax and accounting requirements, copyright and rights-management records, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, legal compliance, security and legitimate operational purposes.

Some information may remain after account closure where retention is legally or operationally necessary.

## 7. Security
We use commercially reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information and risks involved. No internet-based system can be guaranteed completely secure.

## 8. Cookies
Cookies and similar technologies may be used for authentication, dashboard functionality, preferences, analytics, security, fraud prevention, reporting and marketing where applicable. See the Cookie Policy for details.

## 9. User Rights
Subject to applicable law, users may have rights to access, correct, update or request deletion of personal information, and may have rights relating to marketing communications and cookies.

Requests may be limited where retention is required for royalties, publishing, tax, legal claims, fraud prevention, security, contractual obligations or other lawful purposes.

## 10. Children
The Services are not intended for children where use would be unlawful without appropriate parental or guardian involvement. We do not knowingly collect personal information in violation of applicable children's privacy laws.

## 11. Third-Party Services
Third-party platforms and service providers may process information under their own privacy policies. Their practices are not controlled entirely by Tunewave.

## 12. Data Processing Agreements
Where Tunewave processes personal data on behalf of a business customer acting as a controller, the parties may enter into a separate Data Processing Agreement addressing processor obligations, security, subprocessors, data-subject requests, breach response, deletion/return and international transfers.

## 13. Policy Changes
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be made available through the website, dashboard or other appropriate communication channels.
