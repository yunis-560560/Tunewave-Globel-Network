# COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

**Tunewave Globel Network**  
**Effective Date:** September 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 2026

## 1. Professional Use
Users must use Tunewave Services professionally, lawfully and honestly and provide accurate information.

## 2. Rights and Ownership
Only submit Content that you own or validly control, or for which you have the necessary licenses and permissions. You are responsible for contributor approvals, samples, beats, artwork, lyrics, publishing rights, neighboring rights, synchronization permissions and monetization rights applicable to your Content.

## 3. Prohibited Content and Conduct
The Platform must not be used for:
- Copyright, trademark or other intellectual-property infringement.
- Fraudulent ownership, metadata or monetization claims.
- Illegal or criminal activity.
- Terrorist or violent-extremist content.
- Child sexual exploitation or other unlawful exploitation.
- Threats, harassment or unlawful abuse.
- Impersonation or deceptive identity practices.
- Phishing, hacking or unauthorized system access.
- Payment fraud or chargeback abuse.

## 4. Artificial Streaming and Engagement
Artificial or manipulated streams, bots, click farms, fake engagement, incentivized fraudulent streaming, purchased fake followers, playlist manipulation, traffic manipulation, ad fraud and other attempts to manipulate platform metrics are prohibited.

## 5. Content ID and CMS Integrity
Users must not submit false ownership claims, duplicate references, manipulated fingerprints, abusive whitelist requests, false conflicts or other material intended to improperly capture monetization.

## 6. Metadata
Metadata must be accurate, complete and non-deceptive. Do not use misleading artist names, false credits, keyword manipulation, fabricated collaborations, false ownership information or duplicate releases to manipulate discovery or monetization.

## 7. Publishing
Publishing submissions must accurately identify writers, composers, lyricists, ownership shares and relevant registrations. Conflicting or unauthorized registrations are prohibited.

## 8. Platform Security
Users must not bypass security, reverse engineer protected systems, scrape restricted data, abuse APIs, disrupt services or attempt unauthorized access.

## 9. Enforcement
Depending on the circumstances, Tunewave may reject Content, remove Content, freeze royalties, suspend payouts, disable monetization, release claims, suspend publishing, restrict dashboard access, disable APIs, terminate accounts or permanently restrict access.

## 10. Reporting
Suspected violations may be reported to support@tunewavemusic.in. Reports should contain sufficient information for investigation.

## 11. No Guaranteed Availability
Tunewave does not guarantee permanent distribution, monetization, Content ID access, publishing access, platform approval or uninterrupted availability.

## 12. Updates
These Guidelines may be updated through the website, dashboard, email or other appropriate notices.
