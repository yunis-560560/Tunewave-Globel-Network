# COPYRIGHT, TAKEDOWN & CONTENT ID POLICY

**Tunewave Globel Network**  
**Effective Date:** September 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 2026

## 1. Purpose
This Policy explains how Tunewave handles copyright complaints, takedown requests, Content ID disputes, ownership conflicts and related rights-management matters.

## 2. Rights Responsibility
Users are solely responsible for ensuring that Content submitted to Tunewave is fully owned or properly licensed and that all permissions required for distribution, publishing, monetization, YouTube, Content ID, lyrics, social exploitation and other activated Services have been obtained.

## 3. Prohibited Rights Abuse
Users must not submit infringing Content, unauthorized samples, compositions, lyrics, artwork or other material, or make fraudulent ownership, takedown, metadata, monetization or duplicate claims.

## 4. Copyright Complaint
A complaint should include, where applicable:
1. Complainant's legal name and contact information.
2. Identification of the copyrighted work.
3. Identification or URL/details of the allegedly infringing Content.
4. Evidence of ownership or authority.
5. Explanation of the alleged infringement.
6. Good-faith confirmation.
7. Signature or other required authentication.

Send complaints to **support@tunewavemusic.in**.

## 5. Investigation
Tunewave may investigate ownership, Content ID references, publishing registrations, metadata, duplicate claims, royalty conflicts and other rights issues.

During an investigation, Tunewave may withhold royalties, suspend payouts, freeze monetization, disable claims, remove Content, disable references, restrict accounts or suspend publishing where reasonably necessary.

## 6. Third-Party Decisions
DSPs, YouTube, Content ID systems, collection societies and other platforms may have their own dispute and enforcement procedures. Tunewave may follow or implement applicable third-party decisions.

## 7. Counter-Notification / Dispute Response
A responding party may be asked to provide legal name, contact information, relevant agreements or licenses, registrations, ownership evidence, explanation of the dispute, good-faith confirmation and signature.

Incomplete or unsupported responses may be rejected or may require additional information.

## 8. Private Ownership Disputes
Tunewave may require disputing parties to resolve private ownership or contractual disputes independently and provide appropriate documentation before rights or monetization are restored.

## 9. False or Abusive Complaints
False, malicious or bad-faith complaints are prohibited. Tunewave may reject such complaints and may restrict accounts, freeze royalties, recover losses or take other lawful action.

## 10. Repeat Infringement
Repeated infringement or repeated abuse of copyright, Content ID, monetization or takedown processes may result in suspension, demonetization, termination or permanent restriction.

## 11. No Guarantee
Distribution or registration through Tunewave does not itself establish legal copyright ownership. Tunewave does not guarantee acceptance, monetization, Content ID availability, collection, discoverability or continuity.
