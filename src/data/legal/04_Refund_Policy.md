# REFUND POLICY

**Tunewave Globel Network**  
**Effective Date:** September 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 2026

## 1. General Rule
Payments for Services are generally final and non-refundable once the relevant Service has been activated, submitted, initiated, configured, processed or delivered, except where a refund is approved by Tunewave in writing or required by applicable law.

## 2. Services Generally Non-Refundable After Work Begins
This may include:
- Distribution onboarding, DSP delivery, metadata work, release updates, redelivery, migration and takedown processing.
- Publishing onboarding, registration, metadata processing, rights administration and sub-publishing work.
- YouTube and Content ID activation, fingerprinting, CMS/claim operations, monetization and related processing.
- Dashboard, API, analytics, enterprise onboarding, migration, consulting, support and other operational work.

## 3. One-Time Plans
One-time plans do not create recurring billing unless the applicable commercial terms expressly state otherwise. Activated plans, consumed release capacity, completed onboarding and work already performed are generally not refundable.

## 4. Billing Errors
Tunewave may provide a refund, credit or adjustment for verified duplicate charges, billing errors, technical payment errors or other billing mistakes attributable to Tunewave.

Requests should generally be submitted within 14 days of the relevant transaction, together with information reasonably required for verification.

## 5. Fraud and Abuse
Refunds or credits may be denied where a transaction, account or Content is connected with fraud, artificial streaming, fake traffic, payment abuse, ownership disputes, copyright conflicts, Content ID abuse, metadata manipulation or other prohibited conduct.

## 6. Third-Party Actions
Third-party platform delays, demonetization, royalty adjustments, Content ID decisions, takedowns, investigations, outages or policy enforcement do not automatically create a right to a refund.

## 7. Takedown
Removing Content after distribution or monetization work has begun does not automatically reverse fees for work already performed. Third-party removal timing may vary.

## 8. Enterprise and Custom Services
Refunds for enterprise or custom services are governed by the applicable written order, commercial schedule or enterprise agreement.

## 9. Chargebacks
Chargebacks that are fraudulent or made in bad faith are prohibited. Tunewave may suspend Services, hold royalties, recover amounts, offset balances and pursue available remedies where appropriate.

## 10. Approved Refunds
Approved refunds may normally be returned through the original payment method. Banking, payment processor, currency conversion, tax or other unavoidable charges may affect the amount or timing where permitted by law.
