# MUSIC DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

**Tunewave Globel Network**  
**Effective Date:** September 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 2026

## 1. Agreement
This Music Distribution Agreement (“Distribution Agreement”) governs digital distribution and related monetization Services provided by Tunewave to the Artist, Label or Rights Holder (“Rights Holder”).

Electronic acceptance through the Platform, dashboard, activation flow or other approved method constitutes acceptance of this Agreement.

## 2. Appointment and License
During the applicable Term and Territory, Rights Holder appoints Tunewave as its distribution and monetization partner for the Content submitted under the activated Services.

Rights Holder grants Tunewave the rights necessary to host, reproduce, encode, deliver, distribute, communicate, monetize, administer claims, operate Content ID where activated and provide related Services through DSPs, YouTube, social platforms and authorized partners.

Rights Holder retains ownership of the Content.

## 3. Territory
The standard Territory is worldwide unless the applicable commercial terms specify a narrower territory.

## 4. Content
Content may include sound recordings, music videos, artwork, metadata, thumbnails and promotional assets.

Rights Holder is responsible for accurate metadata and for all necessary rights, licenses and contributor approvals.

## 5. Exclusivity
Where the activated plan is exclusive, Rights Holder will not appoint another distributor or monetization provider for the same rights and Content during the applicable exclusive period, except where the applicable commercial terms expressly permit it.

## 6. Services
Services may include digital DSP distribution, YouTube monetization, Content ID, social monetization, analytics, reporting, release management, metadata administration, integrations and support.

## 7. Commercial Terms
Revenue share, fees, payout thresholds, service modules and other commercial terms are determined by the applicable accepted plan, dashboard configuration, order, commercial schedule or signed agreement.

A signed or expressly accepted custom commercial agreement will prevail over inconsistent standard commercial terms.

## 8. Net Revenue and Royalties
Net Revenue is based on amounts actually received and retained by Tunewave after permitted third-party commissions, taxes, banking/payment/currency costs, refunds, reversals, chargebacks, fraud or artificial-streaming deductions, clawbacks and other agreed adjustments.

General internal overhead such as salaries and ordinary internal operating expenses are not deducted unless a separate commercial agreement expressly provides otherwise.

## 9. Reporting and Payouts
Reports depend on information and payment cycles received from third parties. Payouts may be subject to KYC, tax information, thresholds, reserves, verification, reversals and applicable deductions.

Tunewave may hold, reserve, delay, reverse or offset royalties for fraud, copyright disputes, ownership conflicts, artificial streaming, platform reversals, chargebacks, conflicting monetization claims, legal requirements or material operational risk.

## 10. Artificial Streaming and Fraud
Artificial streams, bots, fake engagement, traffic manipulation, monetization abuse and Content ID abuse are prohibited.

Where suspected, Tunewave may investigate and may suspend or freeze affected Content, claims, monetization and royalties, and may recover amounts attributable to confirmed abuse.

## 11. Warranties
Rights Holder warrants that:
- it owns or validly controls the rights necessary for the Services;
- Content does not knowingly infringe third-party rights;
- samples, beats, artwork, audiovisual materials and other third-party elements are properly cleared;
- metadata and ownership information are accurate;
- it has authority to accept this Agreement.

## 12. Takedown and Termination
For standard Services, Rights Holder may request withdrawal or termination through available dashboard processes. Tunewave will submit applicable takedown requests within the operational timeframe stated for the Service, generally within 10 business days after a valid request.

Third-party DSP processing times are outside Tunewave's control.

Enterprise or fixed-term arrangements may contain different withdrawal and termination provisions.

## 13. Post-Term Activities
After termination or takedown, Tunewave may continue accounting, reconciliation, royalty collection, reporting, claim release and other activities necessary to complete outstanding transactions.

## 14. Third-Party Platforms
DSPs, YouTube, social networks and other third parties control their own platforms, policies, availability and processing times.

## 15. Indemnity
Rights Holder agrees to indemnify Tunewave against claims, losses, penalties and reasonable costs arising from breach of warranties, rights infringement, inaccurate ownership information, unlawful Content, fraud or misuse of Services.

## 16. Liability
To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, Tunewave excludes indirect and consequential losses. Subject to applicable law and contractual exceptions, aggregate liability relating to relevant Content is generally limited to Net Revenue actually received and retained by Tunewave from that Content during the six months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

## 17. Policy Compliance
Rights Holder must comply with the Terms & Conditions, Community Guidelines, Copyright/Takedown Policy, Privacy Policy, Refund Policy and applicable platform requirements.

## 18. Changes
Non-material operational updates may be implemented through publication or dashboard notice. Material changes to accepted revenue share, exclusivity, fixed term or other protected commercial terms will require affirmative acceptance where required.

## 19. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. The parties will attempt good-faith resolution before formal proceedings. Where arbitration applies, it will be conducted under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. The arbitration seat is Jaipur, Rajasthan, unless the applicable signed commercial agreement provides otherwise.

## 20. Electronic Records
Account acceptance, dashboard records, timestamps, IP and access logs, commercial configurations, payment records, catalogue submissions and related electronic records may be retained as evidence of the Agreement and Services.
